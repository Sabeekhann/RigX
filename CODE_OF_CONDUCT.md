# Code of Conduct

RIGX is an open-source engineering project. We want technical discussion to be rigorous, direct, and welcoming to people with different levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors and users with respect.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and trade-offs rather than people.
- Be specific and constructive when reporting problems.
- Respect privacy: do not post another person's private prompts, source code, credentials, or identifying data.
- Give credit for others' work and avoid misrepresenting contributions.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope even when you disagree with them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted personal attacks;
- sexualized or demeaning language;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberately disrupting issues, reviews, or community spaces;
- using project channels to distribute malware, stolen credentials, or confidential data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban accounts when behavior materially harms the project or its community.

If a conduct report contains sensitive information, contact the repository owner privately rather than opening a public issue.

This policy applies to the RIGX repository and project-managed community spaces.
